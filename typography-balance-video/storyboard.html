02 · Refined concept

One physical metaphor instead of a list of tips.

Every word has weight. We open on a layout that's literally sliding off the screen, drop its heaviest word onto a chrome balance beam, then fix the tilt with four levers: size, weight, color, space. A readout counts the tilt down from 14.0° to 0.0°. When the beam sits still, the lesson is done.

Original

The Art of Typography Balance

A topic statement as the hook, then definition, tips and outro. Reads like a carousel played as video.

Refined

Type Has Weight

A diagnosis as the hook (“isn't ugly, it's unbalanced”), one metaphor that stays on screen, four satisfying UI interactions, a numeric scoreboard and an ending that loops into the first frame.

The beam

Your chrome seesaw from “Design Balance”, rebuilt so words can sit on it. It doubles as a UI slider.

The tilt readout

A mono chip that turns from pink to lavender as the angle reaches 0.0°.

The Younsse cursor

A named multiplayer cursor makes every fix, so the viewer watches a designer work.

Two-tone headlines

Your lavender-over-white headline pattern, animated line by line on the beat.

Objects in sentences

Your chrome renders sit inside the words as image pills, like the “I took control” reference.

05 · Motion system

Transitions that carry an object across every cut.

No cross-dissolves. Each scene hands a shape, a word or a UI element to the next one.

  1. T1 · 01 → 02Match on action

    Backspace removes “ugly” and the poster's tilt accelerates on the same frame.

  2. T2 · 02 → 03Gravity drop

    “BUILD” falls out of the poster; the camera tilts with it and it lands on the beam.

  3. T3 · 03 → 04UI match cut

    Push in on the heavy word; a selection box snaps around it.

  4. T4 · 04 → 05Shape match

    The resize drag line stretches sideways into the weight slider track.

  5. T5 · 05 → 06Text-to-texture

    The thin strokes of “Light” unravel into lines of paragraph copy.

  6. T6 · 06 → 07Glow to redline

    The glow behind “Saturday” stretches into a vertical spacing redline.

  7. T7 · 07 → 08Lines to axis

    All redlines merge into one center axis that splits the frame.

  8. T8 · 08 → 09Z-space pull-back

    The frame shrinks into one floating card among the iterations.

  9. T9 · 09 → 10Fly-in

    The camera flies into v5 until it fills the frame; the scrub handle enters.

  10. T10 · 10 → 11Callback drop

    The finished card shrinks onto your chrome seesaw, which levels.

  11. T11 · 12 → 01Loop

    “Your” falls into place and the reel restarts without a visible seam.

Motion tokens

UI snap
cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1) · 320–450 ms for selection boxes, chips and panels
Physical
Spring stiffness 220, damping 14, one overshoot, for drops and the beam
Beam settle
Damped swing: 3 oscillations, each 40% of the last, over 0.9 s
Type in
Baseline mask reveal, 30 ms stagger per letter, 60 ms per word
Weight morph
Inter Tight wght axis 900 → 200 over 1.2 s ease-in-out
Camera
Constant 2% push per scene, motion blur on moves above 40 px/frame

Delivery

Format
1080 × 1920, 60 fps for UI smoothness (30 fps fallback), H.264, Rec.709
Safe zones
Key text clears the top 220 px, bottom 420 px and right 140 px. Toggle “Reels safe zones” above to check.
Fonts
Inter Tight (variable), Geist Mono. Both free on Google Fonts.
Assets
Avatar and chrome renders cropped from your “Design Balance” carousel. Re-render the seesaw without the ring and cube if you want words to sit on the real 3D beam.
Music
Minimal electronic at 105 BPM, 20 bars, with a clean downbeat on bar 1 for the loop.
